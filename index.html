Clinical Focus: Treatment of Headaches (Suboccipital, Cervical & Myofascial Care)

Peer-reviewed clinical studies show that targeted myofascial and trigger-point bodywork over the suboccipital, upper-trapezius, sternocleidomastoid, and temporalis muscles supports measurable reductions in headache frequency, pericranial tenderness, and neck strain in adults with tension-type and cervicogenic headaches (Quinn et al., American Journal of Public Health; Moraska et al., Clinical Journal of Pain; Chaibi & Russell, Journal of Manual & Manipulative Therapy). Because bright traffic and driving home after a session can re-trigger cranial and neck tension, our in-home sessions let you rest immediately in your own darkened room. Massage is a supportive wellness service and does not replace physician evaluation or neurological care.
