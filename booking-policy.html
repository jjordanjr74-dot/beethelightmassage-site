Booking & Cancellation

Last updated 2026-10-06

How booking works

Call or text 402-499-2936 — we are available 7 days a week. We use dedicated in-home dispatch with a two-hour arrival window, and you will get a text when your therapist is on the way. Your health intake is completed with your therapist directly, in private.

Changing or cancelling

If your plans change, please call or text us as early as you can so the window can be offered to someone else. We will always try to reschedule you rather than lose your slot.

Arrival windows

Travel across Southern Colorado can vary, so we give a two-hour window rather than a fixed minute. If we are ever running late, we will text you.
