Booking, Payment & Changes

Last updated 2026-10-19

Check before paying

Call or text 402-499-2936 to ask whether your exact address and requested time are available. Ask whether a travel fee applies to your address and package. Listed session rates are $115 for 60 minutes and $160 for 90 minutes; the five-session pass is $475. Confirm pass use and conditions, arrival window, final total, payment method, and written cancellation/refund/rescheduling terms with the practice before you pay. This website does not collect payment, reserve a time, or confirm an appointment.

Changes and cancellations

No cancellation fee or refund promise is stated here. Ask the practice for the applicable terms and get them before payment. If plans change, call or text as early as possible; whether a reschedule or refund is available depends on the terms confirmed for that purchase.

Arrival window

The homepage lists a two-hour arrival window. Confirm the expected window and how the practice will notify you if timing changes.

Health information

Do not send health information through this website or by ordinary text. Ask the practice how any needed intake can be shared privately and securely before providing it.
