Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-06

This is a public information website for Bee the Light Massage Therapy, a mobile massage practice with listed service areas in Pueblo, Pueblo West, Cañon City, Fountain, and Colorado Springs.

Information on this website

This static site has no appointment form or account login. Do not submit medical or other sensitive health details here, by text, or through links on the site. To ask about booking, call or text 402-499-2936. Ask the practice how any necessary intake information is collected, used, secured, and retained before sharing it.

Website providers and measurement

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data to serve the pages. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits and performance. According to Cloudflare, the product does not use visitors’ personal data for analytics and its beacon sets no cookies; see Cloudflare’s data-collection description. This measurement does not measure call or text clicks, booking inquiries, or completed payments. Custom events are not supported by Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site has no advertising pixels.

Messages and payments

If you contact the practice by phone or text, your carrier and the practice may process information in that communication. If you later make a payment, the payment provider will process information under its own terms and privacy notice. Confirm price, availability, travel fee, cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms directly with the practice before paying.

Questions

For questions about this website or its information, call or text 402-499-2936. Do not send health details in your message.
