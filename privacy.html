Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-05

Bee the Light Massage Therapy is a mobile practice serving Pueblo, Pueblo West, Colorado Springs and Fountain. We keep this page short because we keep your data handling simple.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. This site has no contact form, no login, and sets no cookies. Booking happens by phone or text, so there is nothing for you to submit here. The only measurement is the anonymous visitor count described below.

Your health information

Health information you share when you book stays between you and your therapist. It is held confidentially to DORA confidentiality and HIPAA Title II standards, is used only to deliver your care safely, and is not sold or shared for marketing.

Third parties

This site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which processes standard web-server request logs to deliver the page. For a simple visitor count we use Cloudflare Web Analytics: it sets no cookies, stores no personal information, and does not track you across other sites or build a profile of you. There are no advertising pixels and no ad networks on this site.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, and you can ask us to stop contacting you, by calling or texting 402-499-2936.
